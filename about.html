The Founder

Meet the Founder

💼

Olesia Sanchez

Olesia Sanchez founded Drop Cleaning 360 with one simple belief: a truly clean space is built through consistency, attention to detail, and genuine care.

With a background in Economics and more than a decade in Hospitality Public Relations, Olesia learned that exceptional service comes from anticipating clients' needs and exceeding expectations.

Later, her career as an auditor strengthened her skills in organization, quality control, accountability, and process management.

Today, she brings that same level of professionalism to every property Drop Cleaning 360 serves — helping property managers, HOAs, commercial clients, and homeowners maintain spotless, welcoming spaces.

Because at Drop Cleaning 360, we don't just clean — we care. ✨
